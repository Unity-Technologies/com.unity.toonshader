This is expected behavior. Unity Toon Shader is a **lit shader** that reacts to scene lighting, while URP Unlit completely ignores lights and outputs the texture at full brightness. With your light intensity at 0.61, UTS correctly outputs at 61% brightness (0.61 × texture color), which falls below your bloom threshold of 0.7, while Unlit outputs at 100% brightness and blooms normally. You can fix this by either: (1) increasing your light intensity to 1.0 or higher, (2) raising the `Unlit_Intensity` parameter in the toon shader material, or (3) lowering your bloom threshold to ~0.4 to account for the dimmer lighting. Alternatively, are you looking for a true **unlit toon shader** variant that ignores lighting entirely? If so, we could consider adding that as a feature request, though it would lose the directional shading that's core to the toon look.
